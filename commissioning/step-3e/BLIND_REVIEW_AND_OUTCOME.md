This outcome was recorded after all eight frozen calls. The substantive review was completed and frozen **before** the X/Y assignment key was revealed. The user supplied the review finding and subsequently authorized recording the classification; the reviewer's full original notes or transcript were not supplied as a separate artifact. Preserve that distinction.

The reviewer judged Output Set X substantively stronger than Output Set Y, primarily on causal depth, non-obvious consequences, and descendant potential, while approximately tied on constraint coherence and internal consistency. The supplied review did not separately characterize substantive branch diversity; do not infer a finding on that dimension. ## Assignment revealed after review

## Frozen-criteria classification

**Positive diagnostic finding.** In this one controlled paired run, Eidomancer-mediated exploration produced modest but meaningful additional causal depth and non-obvious material useful for descendant exploration relative to direct exploration by the same caller-reported GPT-5.6 Sol intelligence, without an apparent comparable loss of constraint coherence. This does not establish general superiority, improvement in creativity generally, or repeatability. No aggregate creativity score is asserted.

The substantive comparison and the different recoverability/provenance of the arms are separate findings. The blind review did not use provenance advantages to select a stronger substantive output. Neither an external provider-side prompt nor provider-side transport bytes were independently observed.

## Execution and limitations

- The frozen order was followed: direct Coordination and Failure generations, their independent critiques, then Eidomancer Coordination and Failure generations and their independent captured evaluations. There were exactly eight user-mediated calls; no retry, replacement, rescue, or compensating call was made. Both arms used the same structured candidate output contract.
- Model identification (`GPT-5.6 Sol`) was reported by the caller, not independently verified. Actual token/cost usage and provider transport bytes are unknown. Visible submissions and responses are retained per call. The Eidomancer generation adapter-boundary canonical bytes and separate user-visible UI wrappers are retained; the wrapper was needed to mediate each request through the chat interface and is not identical to the canonical envelope alone. The evaluator input is retained as its user-visible text and captured adapter-boundary text.
- The target of comparison is the combined constrained branch/state/evaluation workflow. The once-daily ten-minute inscription-room rule is narrative-only, evaluated qualitatively; R0's three-exchange ceiling is checked mechanically. No real-world empirical inference follows from fictional outcomes.
- Both candidates in the Eidomancer arm passed R0, both captured evaluations had `retain` verdicts, and both branches received retained audit records. The content-addressed Crystal was created because the frozen retention procedure warranted retaining the evaluated bounded exploration. Its source scope describes user-mediated adapter-boundary evidence; the Crystal is not the direct-arm output or the blind comparison finding.
- This diagnostic has one world, one paired run, two predetermined aims and one caller-reported model; the outcome is sensitive to prompt mediation, sampling, and human substantive judgment. The generation UI wrappers convey the canonical envelope and explanatory instructions while direct prompts convey the same premise and aims as text. This presentational difference and unavailable exact provider transport/usage limit causal attribution; no calls were added to compensate.
