The canonical resolved-context envelope makes Eidomancer visible submissions substantially longer, despite materially equivalent domain information and equal call counts. Input size is measurable; provider-side processing, actual inference tokens, cost, latency, and token accounting are unavailable. Therefore this experiment does **not** demonstrate equal inference cost. The longer Eidomancer inputs cannot explain ordinary-arm wins as ordinary resource superiority, but they could confound the World 3 Eidomancer advantage. Blind reviewers saw neither call order nor these sizes.

Potential world-specific counterexamples remain important: the kiln shortfall depends on candidate-introduced rod requirements and lack of substitutes; the lagoon's supply effects depend on unspecified transport alternatives, reed establishment, and patch obstruction; the burrow has no defined behavioral implications for solitude or mixed occupancy. No post-critique revision, rescue call, changed fixture, or extra generation occurred. Reviewer independence is limited to separate fresh chat contexts with the caller as mediator; independent human identity or model identity was not verified.
