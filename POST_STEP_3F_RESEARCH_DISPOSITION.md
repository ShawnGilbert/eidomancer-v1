# Post-Step-3F research disposition

Step 3E gave positive diagnostic evidence against a single direct baseline. Step 3F compared Eidomancer with a stronger ordinary multi-call agentic process using equal call opportunities across three fresh bounded fictional worlds. Under its frozen blind criteria, the ordinary agent was substantively stronger in two worlds; Eidomancer had a qualified structural advantage in one. Step 3F is **negative diagnostic evidence for recurrence of the substantive agent-exploration advantage**, not proof that Eidomancer is generally inferior. Equal inference cost was not established.

The present evidence does not justify developing Eidomancer primarily to improve an advanced LLM's substantive exploratory intelligence. That hypothesis is **parked**, not disproven, pending materially new evidence or a materially different mechanism.

Separate demonstrated properties remain: bounded states and explicit constraints; truthful separation of mechanical checks, evaluator judgments, and unverified constraints; captured generation and evaluation provenance; immutable lineage and audits; reproducible Crystal reference closure; and preservation of failed or negative exploration. These properties concern attribution and recoverability. They do not establish increased intelligence or creativity.

**Soliadic discovery from source material** is a separate, untested hypothesis: can bounded transformation preserve a traceable path from `SOURCE → CLAIM → INFERENCE → DISPLACEMENT → EMERGENT CONSTRUCT` without attributing an emergent construct to its source? This question does not reinterpret the Step 3F outcome or authorize further engine implementation.
